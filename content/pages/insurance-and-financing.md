## Insurance we accept

We are in network with most PPO dental plans, including:

<ul class="check-list">
<li>Fernwick Dental PPO</li>
<li>Tallgrass Mutual Dental</li>
<li>Silverbrook Dental Alliance</li>
<li>Pinecrest Benefit Group</li>
<li>Oriel Dental Plans</li>
<li>Amberly Dental PPO</li>
</ul>

Do not see your plan? Call us anyway. We file claims with almost every PPO plan as an out-of-network provider, and many plans still pay a good share of the cost. We do not currently accept HMO or Medicaid dental plans.

## How we handle your insurance

1. **Before your visit,** we confirm your coverage and tell you what to expect.
2. **At your visit,** you pay only your estimated share.
3. **After your visit,** we file the claim for you. If the plan pays differently than expected, we settle the difference and explain why.

## Paying for treatment

We take cash, debit and all major credit cards, as well as health savings and flexible spending account cards. For larger treatment plans, we can split the cost into monthly payments with no interest over up to six months. Ask our front desk to set it up.

## Common questions

<details class="faq">
<summary>Will you tell me what my insurance covers before treatment?</summary>
<p>Yes. For anything beyond a routine visit, we send a pre-treatment estimate to your plan and share the answer with you before booking.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What happens when my plan resets in January?</summary>
<p>Most plans reset their yearly maximum on January 1. If you have treatment planned, we can help you split it across two plan years to get the most from both.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I use the membership plan with insurance?</summary>
<p>The membership plan is for patients without dental insurance. If you lose coverage, you can join any time.</p>
</details>
