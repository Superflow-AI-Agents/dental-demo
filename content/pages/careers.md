## Why people stay

Our average team member has been with us for more than six years. We think that is because of a few simple things: realistic schedules, no production quotas for hygienists, and managers who still work chairside.

## Benefits

<ul class="check-list">
<li>Medical, dental and vision coverage for you and your family</li>
<li>Paid time off from your first month, plus your birthday off</li>
<li>Retirement plan with a 4 percent match</li>
<li>Yearly continuing education allowance</li>
<li>Four-day weeks for most clinical roles</li>
</ul>
