## How we started

Dr. Helen Whitfield opened the first Larkmoor Family Dentistry in 2004, in a brick storefront on River Road with one treatment chair and a borrowed front desk. She had worked in busy clinics where patients were in and out in fifteen minutes and rarely understood what was done or why. She wanted the opposite.

More than twenty years on, the practice has grown into several neighborhood offices across town, but the first rule has not changed. Every recommendation comes with a reason, a written estimate and time to think.

## What we believe

<div class="grid-3">
<div class="card">
<h3>Explain everything</h3>
<p>You see your own x-rays and photos. We tell you what we see, what can wait and what cannot.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Treat conservatively</h3>
<p>If a small filling will do, you get a small filling. We watch borderline spots instead of drilling them.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Make it easy to come back</h3>
<p>Early mornings, Saturdays, reminders that are actually helpful, and the same hygienist each time when we can.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Our team

Our dentists and hygienists have practiced in Larkmoor for years, and many of them live a few streets from the office where they work. You can [meet the team](/team) and read about each of them.

## In the community

Every spring we run free screenings at Larkmoor elementary schools, and every November our offices collect unopened toothbrushes and toothpaste for the Larkmoor community food pantry.
