# Privacy policy

<p class="note">Effective October 1, 2026</p>

Larkmoor Family Dentistry is a fictional practice used for product demos. This policy describes how this website works. It does not cover real patient records, because there are none.

## What this website collects

**Forms.** The appointment and contact forms on this site send your entry to a test endpoint that replies with a thank-you page. Nothing you type is stored, emailed or shared with anyone.

**Analytics and cookies.** This site uses no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own.

**Server logs.** Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for a short time to keep the service secure and running.

## Health information

Please do not enter medical details into any form on this site. In a real practice, health information is protected under federal privacy law and handled only through secure channels, never through a website contact form.

## Links to other sites

This website does not link to outside services. If that changes, those sites will have their own privacy policies.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
