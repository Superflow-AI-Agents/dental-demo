# Accessibility

We want every patient to be able to use this website and visit our offices with ease. This page explains what we do and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

## Our website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA. In practice, that means:

<ul class="check-list">
<li>Every image has a text description</li>
<li>Pages work with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline</li>
<li>Text and buttons meet contrast guidelines</li>
<li>Headings follow a clear order so screen readers can move through a page</li>
<li>Pages work at 200 percent zoom and on small phone screens</li>
</ul>

## Our offices

Every office has step-free entry and at least one accessible parking space. Treatment rooms have space for a wheelchair beside the chair, and we can move a patient from a wheelchair with help from our trained staff. Service animals are always welcome.

If you need more time, a quieter appointment, written instructions in large print, or a sign language interpreter, tell us when you book and we will arrange it at no cost to you.
