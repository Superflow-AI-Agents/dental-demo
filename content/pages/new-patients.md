## What happens at your first visit

Plan on about 75 minutes. It is a little longer than a routine checkup because we are getting to know your mouth for the first time.

1. **Check-in.** We scan your ID and insurance card and go over your health history.
2. **X-rays and photos.** A full set of digital x-rays and a few intraoral photos, so you can see what we see.
3. **Cleaning.** Your hygienist cleans and polishes your teeth and measures your gums.
4. **Exam.** Your dentist checks every tooth, your bite and your jaw joints, and screens for oral cancer.
5. **Your plan.** If anything needs treatment, you leave with a written plan that lists each step and its cost. Nothing is booked unless you want it to be.

## What to bring

<ul class="check-list">
<li>A photo ID</li>
<li>Your dental insurance card, if you have one</li>
<li>A list of medications and supplements you take</li>
<li>Recent x-rays from your last dentist, if you have them. We can request them for you.</li>
</ul>

## What it costs

With most PPO plans, your first exam, cleaning and x-rays are covered in full. We will check your benefits before you arrive and tell you if anything will not be covered.

Without insurance, the new patient visit with an exam, cleaning and x-rays is $149. If you plan to stay with us, our membership plan covers two cleanings and exams a year, x-rays and a discount on treatment for $29 a month for adults and $19 a month for kids under 14. See [insurance and financing](/insurance-and-financing) for the details.

## Common questions

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I bring my kids to the same visit?</summary>
<p>Yes. We can book family members back to back, or side by side in neighboring rooms when we have the space.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>It has been years since I saw a dentist. Will I be judged?</summary>
<p>Never. Many of our patients come back after a long gap. We start where you are and go at your pace.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do you fill out forms before the visit?</summary>
<p>After you book, we email a short link to fill in your health history at home. If you prefer paper, we will have it ready at the front desk.</p>
</details>
