Surgery can sound daunting, so Dr. Brandt spends real time on planning. He uses 3D imaging to map the bone and nerves before an implant, walks you through each stage on screen, and tells you honestly how long healing will take. On the day, he numbs carefully and talks you through what you will feel. Because implants are placed and restored under one roof, you are not sent elsewhere between steps.

You will usually find him at our [Riverside office](/locations/riverside), where he handles wisdom tooth removal, single implants, implant-supported dentures and traditional dentures. Learn more about [dental implants](/services/dental-implants).

## Education and training

Dr. Brandt earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from a dental school in the Midwest in 2009. He then completed a two-year hospital residency in general dentistry with a strong focus on oral surgery, extractions and implant placement. Since then he has completed hundreds of hours of advanced continuing education in implant surgery, bone grafting and full-arch restorations.

## What patients can expect

- A planning visit with 3D imaging before any surgery
- A clear timeline for each stage, from extraction to final tooth
- Options for extra comfort, including sedation when it suits you
- Written aftercare instructions and a check-in call after every procedure

If you are weighing an implant against a bridge or a denture, he will lay out the trade-offs plainly so you can decide with confidence.

## Outside the office

Dr. Brandt has lived in Larkmoor, Oregon, since 2013. He spends his free time fly fishing the rivers east of town, restoring an old wooden canoe in his garage, and cheering on the high school football team on Friday nights. He also builds birdhouses with his two daughters, which now hang all over their neighborhood.
