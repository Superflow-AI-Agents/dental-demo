With young children, she starts slowly. She lets kids sit in the chair, ride it up and down, and count their own teeth with a mirror before anything else happens. For parents, she explains what she sees in plain words and shares simple habits that make a real difference at home. She also cares for whole families, so parents and kids can often book back to back.

You will usually find her at our [Old Mill office](/locations/old-mill), where she sees children from their first tooth onward, along with plenty of parents and grandparents. Learn more about [kids dentistry](/services/kids-dentistry).

## Education and training

Dr. Natarajan earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from a dental school in the Pacific Northwest in 2014. She then completed a hospital residency in general dentistry with extended rotations in pediatric care, including treating children with special health care needs. She continues to study child behavior guidance and preventive care such as sealants and fluoride.

## What patients can expect

- A relaxed pace, with a "tell, show, do" approach for kids
- Praise, stickers and a small prize at the end of each visit
- Honest, judgment-free advice for parents about snacks, brushing and thumb sucking
- Room for a parent to stay close during the whole visit

If your child has had a hard experience before, let us know when you book. She is glad to schedule a short get-to-know-you visit first.

## Outside the office

Dr. Natarajan lives in Larkmoor, Oregon, with her husband, two children and a very loud beagle. She loves hiking the forest trails outside town, reading mystery novels, and cooking big family dinners from her grandmother's recipes. She also reads stories at the public library's toddler hour once a month.
