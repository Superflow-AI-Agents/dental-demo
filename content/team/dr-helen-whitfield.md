Two decades later, she still works that way. She sits at eye level before an exam, shows you photos of what she sees, and writes out your options so you can take them home and think. Many of her patients have been with her since the early days, and she now cares for their grandchildren too.

You will usually find her at our [Riverside office](/locations/riverside), where she sees patients for checkups, fillings, crowns and the occasional tricky diagnosis that needs a second look.

## Education and training

Dr. Whitfield earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from a dental school in the Pacific Northwest in 1998. She then spent a year in a hospital residency in general dentistry, where she learned to care for patients with complex medical histories. She keeps up with new techniques through continuing education every year, with a special interest in conservative restorations that save as much natural tooth as possible.

## What patients can expect

- A calm, unhurried exam with time for your questions
- A written plan with your options, ranked by what matters most
- Honest advice about what can safely wait
- Numbing gel before every injection, and breaks whenever you ask

If you are nervous, tell her. She is known for talking patients through each step and stopping the moment you raise a hand.

## Outside the office

Dr. Whitfield grew up in Larkmoor, Oregon, and has never wanted to live anywhere else. On weekends she tends a large vegetable garden, swims at the community pool most mornings, and rarely misses the Saturday farmers market. She also volunteers at free dental screening days for local schools.
