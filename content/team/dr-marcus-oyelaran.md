Patients often arrive worried about a root canal. Dr. Oyelaran's goal is to make it feel like any other appointment. He numbs thoroughly, checks in often, and explains each step as he goes, so there are no surprises. Afterward, he will tell you exactly what to expect over the next few days.

You will usually find him at our [Northgate office](/locations/northgate), where he handles everything from a single cracked molar to a full plan for rebuilding a worn smile. Learn more about [crowns and bridges](/services/crowns-and-bridges).

## Education and training

Dr. Oyelaran earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from a dental school on the West Coast in 2012. He went on to complete an advanced program in general dentistry with extra time in endodontics, the care of the tooth's inner pulp. He regularly takes hands-on courses in restorative techniques and digital crown design.

## What patients can expect

- A clear explanation of why a tooth needs treatment, with photos
- Careful numbing, and a check that you are comfortable before he starts
- Restorations shaped to match your bite and the color of your natural teeth
- A follow-up call the day after any root canal

He is happy to talk through the pros and cons of each option, including doing nothing for now, so you can choose what fits your life.

## Outside the office

Dr. Oyelaran moved to Larkmoor, Oregon, with his family in 2016 and quickly fell for the area. He coaches a youth soccer team on Saturday mornings in the fall, bakes bread on Sundays, and spends summer evenings cycling the farm roads north of town. He also plays bass in a small community jazz group that performs at the library each spring.
