Luis sees a lot of families, so he is used to adjusting his approach from one chair to the next. With younger patients, he names each tool, lets them feel the polisher on a fingernail, and keeps things moving. With adults, he takes time to talk about sensitivity, staining or bleeding gums, and works with you on a plan to improve them. He is fluent in English and Spanish and is happy to care for patients in either language.

You will usually find him at our [Old Mill office](/locations/old-mill), where he cleans teeth for patients of every age, from toddlers to grandparents. Learn more about [cleanings and exams](/services/cleanings-and-exams).

## Education and training

Luis earned his degree in dental hygiene from a college program in the Pacific Northwest in 2018 and became a registered dental hygienist the same year. He has completed additional training in sealants, fluoride treatments and caring for patients with braces. He keeps learning each year through continuing education in preventive care for children.

## What patients can expect

- A friendly, step-by-step cleaning with no surprises
- Sealants and fluoride for kids when they will help
- Plain-words advice on brushing, flossing and diet
- Care in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer

If your child is anxious, ask for Luis when you book. He is great at turning a nervous first visit into a proud one.

## Outside the office

Luis lives in Larkmoor, Oregon, with his wife and their young son. He plays in a weekend adult soccer league, grills a mean carne asada for the neighbors, and spends quiet evenings tending a small garden of chili peppers. He also volunteers at the community center, teaching kids how to brush with a giant toothbrush.
