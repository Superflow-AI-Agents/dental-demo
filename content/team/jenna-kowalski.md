Jenna works with a light touch and talks you through every step. She measures your gums, explains what the numbers mean, and shows you on a mirror where plaque likes to hide. Instead of a lecture, you leave with one or two practical changes that fit your routine. If an area is tender, she will numb it first and slow down.

You will usually find her at our [Northgate office](/locations/northgate), where she sees adults and teens for regular cleanings and gum care. Learn more about [cleanings and exams](/services/cleanings-and-exams).

## Education and training

Jenna earned her degree in dental hygiene from a community college program in Oregon in 2015 and has been a registered dental hygienist ever since. She has completed extra training in gum disease treatment, including deep cleanings, and is certified to give local anesthesia. Each year she takes continuing education courses on periodontal health and patient comfort.

## What patients can expect

- A thorough, gentle cleaning at a pace that suits you
- Gum measurements explained in plain words
- Simple, realistic tips you can start that night
- The same familiar face at each visit, whenever your schedule allows

If you have not had a cleaning in a while, there is no judgment. Jenna will simply start where you are and help you build from there.

## Outside the office

Jenna grew up on a small farm just outside Larkmoor, Oregon. These days she spends weekends trail running, camping with friends, and tending her backyard chickens. She is also a devoted quilter and donates a handmade quilt to the county fair raffle every summer.
